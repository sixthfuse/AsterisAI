Post-graduation work permit (PGWP) eligibility

IRCC announced on September 18, 2024 several major changes to the International Student Program in Canada including PGWP eligibility requirements. Students who have submitted a study permit application on or after November 1, 2024, must have graduated in an eligible field of study.

Select the filter feature below to find BCIT programs that are PGWP eligible and meet IRCC’s eligible field of study requirement (CIP). Students should ensure that they review and meet all other general IRCC PGWP eligibility criteria. Issuance of a PGWP is at sole discretion of IRCC.

Visit Flexible Learning Programs, for PGWP eligible programs in Flexible Learning.
